    How this guide was made

    Candidate names, parties and hometowns come from the Iowa Secretary of State's official candidate list (updated August 19, 2026, after the deadline for parties to fill ballot vacancies). Candidates appear in the order that list uses.

    Positions are short summaries of what candidates have said or how they have voted, drawn from Iowa news outlets and the candidates' own statements. Quoted words are the candidate's. Every summary links to its source. "No stated position found" means we didn't find one in the coverage we reviewed, not that the candidate has none.
